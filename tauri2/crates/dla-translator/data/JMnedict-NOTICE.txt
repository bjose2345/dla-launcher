DLA Launcher spells Japanese character names with the help of a trimmed copy
of JMnedict, the Japanese Proper Names Dictionary.

JMnedict is the property of the Electronic Dictionary Research and
Development Group (EDRDG), and is used in conformance with the Group's
licence:

  https://www.edrdg.org/edrdg/licence.html

It is licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike Licence
(V4.0):

  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

About JMnedict: https://www.edrdg.org/enamdict/enamdict_doc.html

DLA Launcher keeps only the entries for surnames and given names, with their
readings written in romaji (names.tsv.gz). That file is distributed under
the same licence. tauri2/scripts/build-name-list.py in the DLA Launcher
source rebuilds it from JMnedict.xml.gz.
